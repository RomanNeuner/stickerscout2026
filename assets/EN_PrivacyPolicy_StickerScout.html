1 Data We Collect

Account & Authentication

Email address (via Google/Apple login), name (optional), unique user ID (Firebase Auth).

Purpose: Account management
Legal basis: Contract performance — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR

Collection Data

Which stickers/cards you own, need, or have as duplicates. Scan history and trade offers.

Purpose: Core app functionality
Legal basis: Contract performance — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR

Location Data

Approximate location (city level) for finding nearby trade partners. Only requested when using the trade feature. Not stored permanently.

Purpose: Trade exchange
Legal basis: Consent — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR

Push Notifications

Anonymous push token and your language/region preference for sending match reminders and trade alerts.

Legal basis: Consent — Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR

Camera

✅ Camera images are NOT stored or transmitted. OCR processing happens entirely on your device using Google ML Kit.
Purpose: Sticker & card scanning only

In-App Purchases

Payment data is processed exclusively by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made. No payment card data is stored by NCN.

Usage Data

Daily scan count, app version, OS and device type (anonymized), crash reports (no personal data).

Legal basis: Legitimate interest — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR
